# Raw annual LSWT is the primary warming representation

## Context

The project needs one stable basis for long-term warming and changing local warming speed.

## Choice

Use raw annual-mean reconstructed LSWT for full-period Theil–Sen warming, endpoint-aligned trailing 10-year Theil–Sen local speed, and the Sen trend of that speed sequence. Call the last quantity *warming-speed change*, not physical acceleration.

## Alternatives and evidence

STL trends and adjacent-difference formulations were considered. STL remains useful for the PCA input, but the raw annual representation best preserves one consistent primary metric and is directly inspectable in the descriptive draft.

## Consequences and reversibility

All main warming claims use raw annual LSWT. Changing this decision would require rerunning the metric comparison and revising the analysis contract.
