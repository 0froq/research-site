# Teleconnection results remain descriptive spatial associations

## Context

The retained JJA NAO/AO analysis relates detrended summer LSWT sensitivity to the PCA trajectory space, but it does not establish a heat-budget pathway.

## Choice

Describe the result as a reproducible spatial association or co-location. Do not state that NAO/AO causes a principal component, a warming pathway, or individual-lake temperature change.

## Alternatives and evidence

The association is checked with spatial hold-outs, alternate grids, leave-one-continent-out, and leave-one-decade-out tests. These support reproducibility of the association, not causal attribution.

## Consequences and reversibility

The result can contextualise the trajectory structure, but cannot become a mechanism claim without independent physical drivers and an explicit attribution design.
