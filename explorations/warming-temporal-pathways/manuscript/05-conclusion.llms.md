# Conclusion

## Global lake warming has a temporal geography

This study addresses a limitation of global lake-warming summaries: a long-term LSWT trend quantifies net change but does not retain the temporal history through which that change occurred. Using reconstructed 1981–2020 annual LSWT for 92,245 lakes, we paired forty-year Theil–Sen slopes with endpoint-aligned trailing ten-year Sen rates. The resulting records show that comparable net warming can arise through different histories of strengthening, weakening or changing local warming rate. Reporting a single long-term trend therefore captures an essential part of global change, while leaving out timing that can matter for subsequent ecological and physical comparison.

> 本文的首要贡献是把全球湖泊净增温与其形成过程分开表述。四十年 slope 描述净变化，端点对齐速率保留变化发生的阶段。

The pathway view is supported by two complementary forms of evidence. First, the lake-level rate histories remain heterogeneous among lakes with similar long-term warming. Second, equal-area PCA condenses the low-frequency cell-level trajectories into a leading background and a secondary timing plane. The score fields are spatially continuous, and their relation to long-term warming shows that the secondary timing information is not exhausted by net temperature displacement. Together, these results define pathway diversity as an empirical feature of the global record rather than a set of predefined lake types.

> 证据链有两层：相近净增温下的局地速率历史仍不同；等面积 PCA 将这些差异组织为主背景和次级时间平面。路径以连续坐标表达。

Spatial balance is central to that conclusion. The fitted PCA axes represent occupied equal-area cells instead of a covariance pattern weighted by the number of observed lakes in each region. Individual lakes are subsequently projected onto those fixed axes. This separation lets the analysis describe both a globally balanced trajectory space and lake-level positions within it. It also makes the geographic interpretation auditable: the mapped cell scores are the same spatial units that determine the principal components.

> 等面积 cell 先定义全球轨迹空间，再把各湖泊投影到固定轴。地图与 PCA 的空间估计对象一致，地理含义可追溯。

The robustness evidence sets the strength of each retained result. PC1 recurs after continental and temporal omissions, changes in grid resolution, mean-versus-median cell aggregation and removal of the sparsest cells. It can therefore be used as the leading low-frequency background of the observed period. The PC2–PC3 plane recurs across many spatial perturbations and retains information about timing beyond PC1, but its orientation is less stable when North America, Europe or either end of the record is removed. The paper treats this secondary structure as a bounded joint plane, not as two immutable global modes. That distinction is part of the conclusion rather than a technical qualification confined to the sensitivity analysis.

> PC1 的跨扰动复现支撑主背景结论。PC2–PC3 保留为有空间与时间支持边界的联合平面，不能拆成两条固定全球轴。

This framework can support several next uses without claiming that it already explains lake heat budgets. Ecological analyses can compare lakes that have similar cumulative warming but contrasting timing histories. Process studies can ask whether radiation, wind, humidity, ice state or basin morphology align with a trajectory coordinate after their covariation is specified in advance. Monitoring programmes can select sites that span the continuous pathway field, thereby retaining both the leading global background and the regional timing contrasts. Each use relies on the coordinates as a common descriptive language for combining the present temperature records with independent data.

> 路径坐标可连接生态比较、能量收支研究和监测设计。它提供共同描述语言，独立数据可在同一时间地理框架中检验。

The prior-summer NAO/AO screen narrows one such future question. Its spatial association fields co-locate with secondary trajectory geometry after the geographic and morphological comparison, but the screen does not distinguish circulation forcing from intermediate heat-flux pathways or local confounding. The next test is consequently specific: a predeclared attribution design with seasonally matched energy-balance variables, independent hold-outs and explicit representation of ice state and basin morphology. The contribution of the present work is to identify the temporal contrasts and spatial settings that such a design must explain.

> NAO/AO 结果提出一个窄而可检验的问题：在预定义留出设计中，能量收支变量能否解释次级路径几何。当前证据只到空间关联。

Global lake warming is thus more than a ranked distribution of long-term trends. It has a temporal geography: common low-frequency change, regional timing contrasts and lake-level histories that are partly shared and partly distinct. Retaining that geography alongside long-term warming provides a stronger descriptive basis for deciding which lakes to compare, which regions to monitor and which physical explanations require direct test.

> 最终结论：全球湖泊增温同时具有净变化分布与时间地理。长期趋势、路径坐标和稳健性边界应共同报告。
