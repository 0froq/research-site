# Temporal pathways of global lake warming

Manuscript workspace

This directory is the formal-paper layer of the project. It turns retained evidence into a manuscript. Exploratory analysis, durable data preparation, source-note extracts and supplementary sensitivity figures remain in their respective project layers.

> 本目录是正式论文层：把保留证据收敛为可投稿手稿。不放探索分析、持久数据处理、文献摘录或补充敏感性图。

## Central claim

Global lake warming is widespread, and lakes differ in both the magnitude and the temporal pathway of warming. Spatially balanced PCA identifies a common low-frequency background and recurring secondary timing structures. These structures are spatially organised continuous coordinates.

> 全球湖泊增温广泛，差异同时存在于幅度和时间路径。空间平衡 PCA 识别共同低频背景与可重复的次级时间结构，并以连续空间坐标呈现。

## Agreed writing workflow

The manuscript follows a fixed evidence route. Independent source evidence is recorded in [Paper notes](../../../notes/papers/index.llms.md); its relationship to this question is recorded in the [Literature map](../../../explorations/warming-temporal-pathways/literature/index.llms.md). Each Introduction claim must map to a reviewed source and its scope. Results are written around retained figures: global kinematics, spatially balanced trajectory structure, stability, and the circumscribed teleconnection association. Methods serve those results. Discussion interprets their meaning, relation to earlier work and limits; Conclusion then consolidates the contribution, decisive evidence, use and boundary without adding evidence. Seasonal and ice diagnostics remain a modular descriptive branch. Further analysis is added only when it directly resolves a stated evidence gap.

> 章节分工已固定：Discussion 解释结果、与既有研究对话并界定限制；Conclusion 收束贡献、关键证据、使用价值和边界，不加入新证据。季节与冰保持模块化描述支路。
