# PCA diagnostic loadings

## Lower-variance temporal modes

PC4 and PC5 are retained to document the first five fitted PCA modes. They account for 5.7% and 4.6% of represented cell-trajectory variance, respectively. Their loadings are not used to define the primary pathway geometry because their omission stability is weaker than that of PC1 and the PC2–PC3 subspace (Results 05).

> 本页保留 PC4–PC5 的时间载荷，便于核查前五个 PCA 模式。它们不进入主路径结论。

![](pca-diagnostics_files/figure-html/fig-supp-pca-pc45-loadings-1.png)

Figure 1: Temporal loadings for PC4 and PC5. These lower-variance modes are retained for diagnostic completeness and are not primary pathway axes.

The figure is descriptive. PC labels and signs can change under equivalent refits; the retained main-text inference concerns the stable leading axis and the PC2–PC3 subspace, rather than a mechanism assigned to either lower mode.

> 图件为诊断性材料。主文仅对 PC1 与 PC2–PC3 平面作稳健性限定下的描述，不将低阶模式解释为机制。
