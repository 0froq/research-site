# PCA spatial-autocorrelation diagnostic

## Distance-binned score covariance

The correlogram supplements the Moran’s I table in Results 04. Positive values mean that score values in a distance bin are more similar than the full-field average. It documents spatial continuity and does not identify a geographic driver.
