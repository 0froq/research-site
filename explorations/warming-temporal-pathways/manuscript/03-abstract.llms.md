# Abstract

Global lake warming is widespread, yet long-term trends omit when change occurs. We analysed reconstructed 1981–2020 annual lake surface water temperature (LSWT) for 92,245 lakes, combining forty-year Theil–Sen slopes with endpoint-aligned trailing ten-year warming rates. Lakes with similar net warming followed heterogeneous histories of strengthening, weakening and changing local rate. Spatially balanced principal-component analysis of low-frequency trajectories identified a dominant background and a secondary timing plane that formed broad continuous geographic fields. The leading axis recurred across continental and temporal omissions, grid resolutions, cell aggregations and cell-density thresholds. The secondary plane recurred across many spatial perturbations, with weaker agreement after particular continental and endpoint omissions. Prior-summer NAO/AO association fields co-located with the secondary geometry after geographic and morphological comparison. These results establish a descriptive temporal geography of global lake warming and provide coordinates for ecological comparison, monitoring and future process-based attribution; the climate association does not identify a circulation mechanism.

> 本文以 92,245 个湖泊的 1981–2020 年 LSWT 记录，结合四十年净变化与端点对齐十年速率。结果给出全球湖泊增温的时间地理：主背景、具有空间组织的次级时间平面，以及后者与夏季 NAO/AO 关联场的共定位。遥相关部分仅提出后续过程检验目标。
