# Terminology ledger — temporal pathways of global lake warming

Internal editorial control document. It records the intended meaning of terms
that carry the manuscript's central claims; it is not rendered with the
manuscript.

| Preferred term | Definition and reporting rule | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| lake surface water temperature (LSWT) | Satellite-reconstructed annual surface-water temperature. Expand at first use. | lake temperature when the surface boundary matters |
| long-term warming | Forty-year Theil–Sen slope of raw annual LSWT, reported in °C decade−1. | PCA trend; acceleration |
| local warming rate | Endpoint-aligned trailing ten-year Theil–Sen slope of raw annual LSWT, reported in °C yr−1. | independent decadal observations |
| local-rate tendency / warming-speed change | Sen slope across the valid local-rate sequence, reported in 10−3 °C yr−2. An operational summary of directional change in local rates, not instantaneous physical acceleration. | acceleration without qualification |
| trajectory / pathway | A continuous temporal history or its coordinate in the fitted low-frequency space. These terms do not define natural lake classes. | pathway type; trajectory class |
| equal-area PCA | PCA fitted to one baseline-centred low-frequency trajectory per occupied equal-area cell; individual lakes are projected afterwards. It estimates covariance among represented occupied cells. | globally representative lake-average PCA |
| PC1 | Leading low-frequency background, interpreted as a one-dimensional axis after robustness checks. | universal trajectory |
| PC2–PC3 plane | Joint secondary timing geometry. Its internal axes may rotate or exchange rank, so use rotation-invariant magnitude for spatial comparisons where possible. | two fixed global modes |
| climate-context screen | Descriptive, spatially structured association screen for candidate follow-up. It does not attribute forcing or identify a heat-budget mechanism. | teleconnection mechanism; circulation-to-lake pathway |
