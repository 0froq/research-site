Discussion

A global warming signal contains diverse temporal histories

The central result is straightforward: widespread positive long-term LSWT trends coexist with markedly different histories of decadal warming rate. The 40-year Theil–Sen slope identifies how far a lake moved between the beginning and end of the record. It does not preserve the order of changes within the record. Results 01 and 02 show why that lost information matters. At comparable net warming, some lakes experienced stronger late-period local rates, others weaker late-period rates, and many showed histories for which a single monotonic tendency is only a compact summary. This pattern occurs across the global population rather than within a small collection of case-study lakes.

核心发现：全球长期增温很广泛，形成同一净增温的过程却多样。长期 slope 保留终点差值；局地速率历史保留变化发生的阶段。

This contribution extends, rather than replaces, previous evidence for period-dependent lake warming. A global study of 155 lakes compared rates before and during the late-1990s to early-2010s slowdown (Winslow et al. 2018), and Central European observations documented an abrupt late-1980s shift (Woolway et al. 2017). Our analysis supplies a common endpoint-aligned rate record for 92,245 lakes, allowing the distribution of pathways to be evaluated over the same 1981–2020 interval. The analysis therefore avoids treating one globally chosen breakpoint as the only temporal contrast of interest. It also shows that a shared total temperature change provides a weak constraint on the timing of that change.

与既有时期比较相比，本文使用全记录连续端点的十年速率。重点从某个固定断点转向每个湖泊的完整速率历史。

The pathway perspective changes the questions that can be asked of lake warming. A net trend remains essential for describing cumulative exposure and for comparing global distributions. A local-rate history adds information about when a lake entered a period of rapid change, when a previous rate weakened, and whether these episodes offset over four decades. Those distinctions are relevant to ecological exposure because ice, stratification, oxygen and habitat conditions respond to seasonal and multiyear temperature states (Woolway et al. 2020; Kraemer et al. 2021). The present data do not connect each trajectory to a biological response. They provide a temperature framework in which that connection can be tested with temporally matched ecological records.

路径表述扩大了可提问题的范围：净变化适合累计暴露，速率历史适合定位快速变化的阶段。生态后果需要与独立生态观测共同检验。

This distinction also changes the appropriate unit of comparison. A lake that has the same 40-year slope as another lake may have entered its rapid-warming period decades later, or may have reached a similar net change after an early episode followed by a slower phase. Grouping lakes only by total trend can therefore mix records with different temporal exposure histories. The pathway coordinates offer a way to define comparison sets that retain this history, while the raw annual series remain available for studies that need the original temperature record rather than a reduced representation.

相同长期 slope 的湖泊可能在不同阶段经历快速变化。路径坐标可用于构造保留时间历史的比较组；原始年序列仍是需要逐年温度时的依据。

Spatial organisation is continuous and geographically structured

The temporal differences were spatially organised. Equal-area cell PCA found a dominant low-frequency background and a recurring secondary PC2–PC3 timing plane, while score maps formed broad continuous fields. The associated Moran’s I values and correlograms show that the maps contain spatial dependence beyond visual impression. This result has a practical interpretation: global lake warming cannot be reduced to one common trajectory plus unstructured lake-to-lake residual variation. Regional coherence is part of the empirical description and should be retained when future studies select comparison sites, aggregate ecological impacts or build spatial prediction models.

路径差异形成连续空间场，Moran’s I 和 correlogram 为地图提供量化支撑。全球变化不能概括为一条共同曲线加随机残差。

The continuous geometry also places a limit on categorical interpretation. PCA scores describe locations in a covariance space. They do not identify intrinsic lake classes, and the secondary axes can exchange order when the data are perturbed. The PC2–PC3 magnitude is more stable for spatial description because it remains unchanged under a rotation of the two axes. This is why the main spatial result focuses on broad score fields and secondary-plane magnitude, rather than assigning lakes to named pathway types. Categories may still become useful in an application with an externally defined management or ecological threshold; such categories would require a separate validation design.

PCA 给出连续坐标。次级单轴可换序，PC2–PC3 振幅更适合表达偏离主轨迹的强度。湖泊类别需要外部阈值和单独验证。

The comparison with global thermal regions clarifies the scope of this result. Thermal-region frameworks classify the form of the annual temperature cycle and provide a strong basis for analysing seasonal change (Maberly et al. 2020). Our PCA uses annual low-frequency trajectories and asks how their timing differs among represented cells during 1981–2020. The two frameworks can be used together: seasonal thermal form may help interpret the physical setting in which a trajectory develops, while trajectory coordinates may identify regions with similar multidecadal timing. Neither framework supplies the other’s estimand.

季节 thermal region 描述年内循环；本文 PCA 描述四十年年均低频轨迹。两者可以互补，不能互相替代。

Spatial balance is consequential here because it specifies whose covariance defines the global trajectory space. The PCA represents occupied equal-area cells, then projects individual lakes onto the fixed cell-derived axes. It therefore avoids allowing regions with many mapped lakes to determine the global modes solely through sample count. This does not make every cell a physical replicate or remove spatial dependence. It gives the geographic estimand a transparent form and makes the cell-level maps directly interpretable as the population used to fit the axes.

等面积平衡明确 PCA 由哪些空间单元定义。它避免高采样区仅凭湖泊数量主导轴，同时不把 cell 当作独立物理重复。

Robustness defines what can be carried forward

The robustness analysis supports the leading trajectory strongly and gives a more qualified account of the secondary plane. PC1 remained closely aligned after continental omission, temporal block omission, changes in grid size, cell aggregation and exclusion of the sparsest cells. Its interpretation as a dominant low-frequency background is consequently supported across several plausible representations of the same data. The median aggregation refit shows that a small number of atypical lakes within a cell do not generate the principal contrasts. The minimum-five-lakes-per-cell refit similarly preserves the matched PC1–PC3 axes after removing 115 sparse cells.

PC1 经大陆、时间、格网、汇总量和 cell 样本数扰动后仍复现。中位数与 n≥5 检验削弱了异常湖和极稀疏 cell 主导结果的担忧。

The secondary result requires more care. The PC2–PC3 plane recurs under most continental omissions and retains its role as a description of timing beyond PC1. Its weakest replication occurs when North America is omitted, and becomes weaker in the minimum-cell-count refit. The temporal block tests also show sensitivity at the beginning and end of the record. These observations do not erase the secondary structure. They specify its evidential status: a recurring spatial timing plane whose exact orientation and strength depend partly on the continental and temporal support of the record. This is a more useful outcome than a binary stable/unstable label, because it identifies the data support needed for replication.

次级平面在多数扰动下出现，北美遗漏、首末十年遗漏和 n≥5 分支给出其最清晰的边界。正文保留这些限制，避免将 PC2、PC3 写成固定全球模式。

These robustness results also guide how the pathway coordinates should be used. PC1 can support comparisons that require a leading low-frequency background across alternative representations. The secondary plane is better used as a joint coordinate for describing regional timing differences, with the reference period and spatial support reported alongside it. Treating PC2 and PC3 as named, independently transportable modes would discard the observed axis exchange and make a stronger claim than the refits support. The resulting framework is therefore deliberately asymmetric: strong inference for the leading background and conditional inference for secondary timing geometry.

稳健性决定使用方式：PC1 可作为主低频背景；次级结构应以联合平面、参考期和空间支持共同报告。轴交换不能被省略。

Climate associations locate a research target

The prior-summer NAO/AO association belongs to this framework as spatial context. The retained fields co-locate with the secondary trajectory geometry more than with PC1 after comparison against geographic and morphological covariates. This result identifies regions where circulation-linked summer sensitivity and pathway timing appear together. It does not resolve the heat budget through which that co-location arises. Atmospheric circulation can affect radiation, wind, humidity, cloud, precipitation and ice conditions, and these influences may interact with basin morphology and local catchment conditions. The current screen contains no independent measures capable of separating these routes.

NAO/AO 结果定位了值得研究的空间背景。热收支环节、因果方向和湖盆条件仍缺独立证据。

The appropriate next step is therefore a designed attribution analysis rather than a larger catalogue of climate indices. Such a study would assemble predeclared, seasonally matched energy-balance variables; define the spatial and temporal hold-outs before model selection; and test whether a candidate pathway remains after morphology, ice state and local forcing are represented. The pathway map developed here supplies a target for that design. It tells an attribution study which temporal contrasts require explanation and where those contrasts occur.

下一步应建立预定义的能量收支与混杂控制设计。本文提供待解释的路径坐标和空间位置。

Scope and limitations

All inferences concern reconstructed lake-surface conditions from GLAST. They do not describe full-water-column heat content or substitute for direct in-situ validation in individual basins. The 1981–2020 record supports a four-decade description and cannot establish behaviour on longer climatic timescales. The overlapping local-rate windows deliberately smooth the annual series and should not be treated as independent observations. PCA supplies a compact covariance description, while mechanisms require independent physical measurements. Finally, the teleconnection result follows a candidate screen; its wording remains limited to reproducible association until it is evaluated in an independent, predeclared analysis.

研究范围是 GLAST 重建的表层状态与 1981–2020 记录。局地窗口、PCA 和遥相关各有明确用途；机制归因仍需独立物理观测。
