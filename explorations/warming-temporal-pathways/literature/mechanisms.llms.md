# Mechanisms and boundaries

Tasks here separate established mechanism context from mechanisms inferred by this exploration. Plausibility alone is not attribution.

## Open evidence tasks

### 01 — Why LSWT is a consequential climate-response variable

Need for  
Introduction, paragraph 1.

Requirements  
support links from LSWT change to ice cover, stratification, oxygen, or thermal habitat;

retain lake-type, depth, and observation conditions;

support exact stated consequence only;

do not extend surface temperature to whole-water-column response without evidence.
