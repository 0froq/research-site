# Novelty and comparators

This map tests the narrow novelty claim: earlier global work establishes widespread and spatially heterogeneous lake warming; the present exploration asks whether the *time paths* of annual warming rates are also spatially organised.

## Open evidence tasks

Need for  
Introduction;

Requirements  
supports for the statement that lake warming is widespread or rapid;

better have multiple, and with actual data;

at least 5 papers;
