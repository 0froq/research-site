# Literature map

These pages hold project-specific evidence tasks. Add a task only when writing or analysis needs evidence, a bounded comparison, or a claim boundary. They do not duplicate source annotations, quotations, or claim extracts; those remain canonical in [Notes](../../../notes/index.llms.md).

> 本目录记录研究写作中出现的证据 task，不是按论文组织的文献表。原文条件、摘录、页码和可复用 claim 均以论文笔记为准。

## How to use the evidence tasks

Task starts in Literature map. Candidate papers enter [Reading queue](../../../notes/reading-queue.llms.md). Reviewed source facts and claims enter a [Paper note](../../../notes/papers/index.llms.md). Then add a scoped, reusable claim with paper-note link and exact source location under task’s `Evidences`. One paper claim may support several tasks. A task stays open when literature gives plausible story but not evidence matching manuscript wording.

> 工作流：Literature map 提出 task；Reading queue 列候选论文；Paper notes 保存审阅后的来源事实与 claim；最后把可支持 task 的、带适用范围的 claim 填回 `Evidences`。一条 claim 可服务多个 task。仅有合理叙事、不足以支撑手稿措辞时，task 保持 open。
