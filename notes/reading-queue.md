---
title: "Reading queue"
---

Candidate papers are recorded here before they become durable paper notes.
Every entry is a bounded reading task. A paper may have several tasks across
explorations or at different times; no task gives it a permanent role.

:::{.column-margin}
## Template

Role now
: method audit

Question
: What known failure conditions apply to this use of PCA?

Expected output
: method limits and required robustness checks

Next
: scan | targeted extraction | audit
:::

Reviewed evidence belongs in [Paper notes](./papers/index.md).
Project-specific roles and questions are
then recorded in the relevant Literature map.
