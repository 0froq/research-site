# Reading queue

Candidate papers are recorded here before they become durable paper notes. Every entry is a bounded reading task. A paper may have several tasks across explorations or at different times; no task gives it a permanent role.

## Template

Role now  
method audit

Question  
What known failure conditions apply to this use of PCA?

Expected output  
method limits and required robustness checks

Next  
scan \| targeted extraction \| audit

Reviewed evidence belongs in [Paper notes](../notes/papers/index.llms.md). Project-specific roles and questions are then recorded in the relevant Literature map.
